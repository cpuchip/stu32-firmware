# Unit 023 on the 4.3" stand-in's S3 (run.sh PORT device/ulp_probe/light.txt; delete build/sdkconfig after
# changing sdkconfig.defaults, which IDF reads only for a fresh config). Measured 2026-10-06:
# - LIGHTSETTLE: the ULP's EXTCOMIN counted 20 edges in 10 s while the chip spent 9.999 s of it in light sleep
#   (esp_pm_dump_locks: SLEEP mode), so the 1 Hz toggle survives automatic light sleep.
# - LIGHT straight after the console lock's release: still 20 edges, but no light sleep in that first block
#   (core 1 holds its CPU lock until its next interrupt); from the next idle period on it sleeps.
# - LIGHTKEY: IO0 low (the host's DTR, 3 s in) wakes it from light sleep, after 2995 ms.
# - LIGHTHALT, the falsifier: the ULP stopped, the count stays flat.
wait PROBE READY 30
send LIGHTSETTLE
expect light: mode LIGHTSETTLE, edges after 30
wait PROBE READY 15
send LIGHT
expect light: mode LIGHT, edges after 30
wait PROBE READY 15
send LIGHTKEY
sleep 3
press0 200
expect lightkey: IO0 woke it 25
wait PROBE READY 15
send LIGHTHALT
expect light: mode LIGHTHALT, edges after 30
wait PROBE READY 15
