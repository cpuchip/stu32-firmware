# abacus's hidden cases for unit 089 (the worksheet, part 1: the rows), written 2026-11-08 from soroban's blueprint at
# stu32-firmware b80103c (work/worksheet-1-rows.md: a-e and the %ROWS dump) and abacus's rulings R1-R4 (#13991): links in
# operand order from the deepest level read to X; a row used twice is linked once; any key that ends a number's entry
# makes its NUM row first; LASTX and ANS make a row linked to the row they bring back. The expected rows come from
# abacus's own model of each op's reads (HP 32SII/35s stack semantics, stack depth 4), not from soroban's census.
#
# Typed numbers, two-argument ops, operand order
H01 | abacus: 5 ENTER 3 + makes three rows; + links both | STU 5 ENTER 3 + | %ROWS=3 %ROW-3=1:NUM:-:5 %ROW-2=2:NUM:-:3 %ROW-1=3:ADD:1,2:8 %LBL:X=3 %LBL:Y=0 %LBL:LASTX=2
H02 | abacus: subtraction, the same shape | STU 5 ENTER 3 - | %ROWS=3 %ROW-1=3:SUB:1,2:2 %LBL:X=3
H03 | abacus: operand order, X older than Y after XY (R1, R3) | STU 5 ENTER 3 XY - | %ROWS=3 %ROW-2=2:NUM:-:3 %ROW-1=3:SUB:2,1:-2 %LBL:X=3
H04 | abacus: a row used twice is linked once (R2) | STU 5 ENTER * | %ROWS=2 %ROW-1=2:MUL:1:25 %LBL:X=2
H05 | abacus: a chain: each result links to the row before | STU 2 ENTER 3 + 4 * | %ROWS=5 %ROW-3=3:ADD:1,2:5 %ROW-2=4:NUM:-:4 %ROW-1=5:MUL:3,4:20 %LBL:X=5 %LBL:Y=0
H06 | abacus: two adds, the second links the first's result and the level below | STU 1 ENTER 2 ENTER 3 + + | %ROWS=5 %ROW-2=4:ADD:2,3:5 %ROW-1=5:ADD:1,4:6 %LBL:X=5
H07 | abacus: T copies down: Z and T hold one row | STU 1 ENTER 2 ENTER 3 ENTER 4 + | %ROWS=5 %ROW-1=5:ADD:3,4:7 %LBL:X=5 %LBL:Y=2 %LBL:Z=1 %LBL:T=1

H15 | abacus: RDN makes no row; the labels move with the values | STU 1 ENTER 2 ENTER 3 ENTER RDN | %ROWS=3 %LBL:X=3 %LBL:Y=2 %LBL:Z=1 %LBL:T=3
