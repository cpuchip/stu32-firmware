# abacus's hidden cases for the worksheet, part 2 (the page), written 2026-11-08 from soroban's blueprint at
# stu32-firmware ad31f3d (work/worksheet-2-page.md with Revision 1) and abacus's rulings (#14041). The expected lines
# come from abacus's own model of the stack (HP 32SII/35s lift, depth 4), part 1's rows (R1-R4) and the blueprint's
# layout, not from soroban's prototype. This file holds only what is the same under all three ways of drawing the
# empty levels (A, B, B' on Michael's card worksheet-levels); h090-A.txt, h090-B.txt and h090-Bp.txt hold the rest,
# and only the one Michael picks is run.
#
# The dump's grammar, ruled here so the build prints it (host only, from screen_lines):
#   %PAGEN=n          the page's lines drawn above the levels: rows, the dropped line, a two-line row counted twice.
#   %PAGE-k=LEFT|VALUE  k = 1 the lowest page line. LEFT is the op's program-line name (list.c's, "%s %s" with its
#                     argument) or an ALG row's text; VALUE is the value as the level lines format it (FIX 4 by
#                     default: 13.0000). Spaces inside either are written _, an empty field is written _ alone. A
#                     two-line row is LEFT|_ above _|VALUE.
#   %LEVELS=...       the levels drawn, top to bottom (TZYX, ZYX, YX, X).
#   %PAGETOP=n|-      the dropped line's count when it is drawn, else -. It is drawn only when n > 0 and the page's
#                     oldest row is on the screen.
#   ▲ (HUP) keeps today's order: the first ▲ shows the newest ALG row, each next one older, stopping at the oldest.
#
# The levels full, so every option draws TZYX and 4 page lines fit
P01 | abacus: held rows are drawn in their levels, not on the page; the rest newest lowest | STU 9 ENTER 8 ENTER 7 ENTER 6 + | %ROWS=5 %ROW-1=5:ADD:3,4:13 %LEVELS=TZYX %PAGEN=2 %PAGE-1=_|6.0000 %PAGE-2=_|7.0000 %PAGETOP=-
P02 | abacus: an op's name at the left; T and Z hold one row, so it is on no page line | STU 9 ENTER 8 ENTER 7 ENTER 6 + 2 * | %LEVELS=TZYX %PAGEN=4 %PAGE-1=_|2.0000 %PAGE-2=+|13.0000 %PAGE-3=_|6.0000 %PAGE-4=_|7.0000
P03 | abacus: six rows not held, four lines: the newest four | STU 9 ENTER 8 ENTER 7 ENTER 6 + 2 * 3 + | %LEVELS=TZYX %PAGEN=4 %PAGE-1=_|3.0000 %PAGE-2=×|26.0000 %PAGE-3=_|2.0000 %PAGE-4=+|13.0000
P04 | abacus: a number being typed is X's and on no page line yet | STU 9 ENTER 8 ENTER 7 ENTER 6 + 5 | %LEVELS=TZYX %PAGEN=2 %PAGE-1=_|6.0000 %PAGE-2=_|7.0000
P05 | abacus: FIX 2 redraws the page's values | STU 9 ENTER 8 ENTER 7 ENTER 6 + FIX2 | %PAGEN=2 %PAGE-1=_|6.00 %PAGE-2=_|7.00
P06 | abacus: an error replaces X's line and the page stays | STU 9 ENTER 8 ENTER 7 ENTER 6 + 0 / | ERR %ERR=DIV_ZERO %LEVELS=TZYX %PAGEN=2 %PAGE-1=_|6.0000 %PAGE-2=_|7.0000
P07 | abacus: the far-back Y of the blueprint's p2: the held rows (2, + 13, √x) on no page line | STU 2 ENTER 3 ENTER 4 * 1 + 7 SQRT | %PAGE-1=_|7.0000 %PAGE-2=_|1.0000 %PAGE-3=×|12.0000 %PAGE-4=_|4.0000
# STO arithmetic makes a row (lean 6 as ruled: A's old row first, then X's; A's old row 0 links X's alone)
P08 | abacus: STO+ on a fresh A links X's row alone, and no level holds it, so it is on the page | STU 9 ENTER 8 ENTER 7 ENTER 6 + STO+:A | %ROWS=6 %ROW-1=6:STOADD:5:13 %LBL:A=6 @A=13 %LEVELS=TZYX %PAGEN=3 %PAGE-1=STO+_A|13.0000 %PAGE-2=_|6.0000 %PAGE-3=_|7.0000
P09 | abacus: L03 as ruled | STU 4 STO:A 2 STO+:A | %ROWS=3 %ROW-1=3:STOADD:1,2:6 %LBL:A=3 @A=6
P10 | abacus: STO- is A - x: A's old row is the left operand | STU 10 STO:A 4 STO-:A | %ROWS=3 %ROW-1=3:STOSUB:1,2:6 %LBL:A=3 @A=6 %PAGEN=1 %PAGE-1=STO-_A|6.0000
P11 | abacus: 35s mode: STO+ makes no row and no page is drawn | MODE35 4 STO:A 2 STO+:A | %ROWS=0 @A=6 %PAGEN=0 %PAGETOP=-
P12 | abacus: 33s mode draws no page | MODE33 9 ENTER 8 + | %ROWS=0 %PAGEN=0 %PAGETOP=-
# ALG: the history is the page's ALG rows
