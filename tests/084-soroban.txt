# Unit 084 (Michael's stack-arithmetic, abacus #12365; work/084-stack-arithmetic.md): in STU's RPN, + - x / y^x and the
# one-argument functions given an expression build a new one, kept as typed. An equation gets the op on both sides; a
# number is written unrounded, pi as the symbol, and with fraction display on as n/d when that fraction is the number.
# Each %XEXPR is the text exactly as built (the lessons quote it). Run with save and load too.
#
# Michael's example, and the stack around it
Q01 | 084: Michael's: the expression in Y, 2 in X, x | STU EQ:3×X^2-4 EQDROP 2 * | %XEXPR=(3×X^2-4)×2
Q02 | 084: 2 in Y, the expression in X | STU 2 EQ:3×X^2-4 EQDROP * | %XEXPR=2×(3×X^2-4)
Q03 | 084: the stack drops as for numbers: Y gets Z | STU 7 ENTER EQ:3×X^2-4 EQDROP 2 * | %XEXPR=(3×X^2-4)×2 Y=7
Q04 | 084: LASTx gets X | STU EQ:3×X^2-4 EQDROP 2 * LASTX | X=2 %YEXPR=(3×X^2-4)×2
Q05 | 084: stored and recalled | STU EQ:3×X^2-4 EQDROP 2 * STO:A CLX RCL:A | %XEXPR=(3×X^2-4)×2
#

Q45 | 084: / with a product on the right: bracketed, as - is (abacus #12410: its plant P1, / not tight on the right, left the vectors green) | STU 6 EQ:2×X EQDROP / | %XEXPR=6÷(2×X)
Q09 | 084: y^x | STU EQ:X+1 EQDROP 2 POW | %XEXPR=(X+1)^2

# An equation gets the op on both sides; a function on both sides can gain roots, as x 0 can (abacus #12192)
Q22 | 084: an equation x 2 | STU EQ:2×X+5=11 EQDROP 2 * | %XEXPR=(2×X+5)×2=11×2
Q23 | 084: an equation x 0 (every X a root now) | STU EQ:2×X+5=11 EQDROP 0 * | %XEXPR=(2×X+5)×0=11×0
Q24 | 084: SIN of an equation (more roots) | STU EQ:2×X+5=11 EQDROP SIN | %XEXPR=SIN(2×X+5)=SIN(11)
Q25 | 084: x² of an equation (X=-8 a root now) | STU EQ:2×X+5=11 EQDROP SQ | %XEXPR=(2×X+5)^2=11^2
Q26 | 084: two equations, side by side | STU EQ:X+1=2 EQDROP EQ:X=3 EQDROP + | %XEXPR=X+1+X=2+3
Q27 | 084: e^x of an equation | STU EQ:X=2 EQDROP EXP | %XEXPR=EXP(X)=EXP(2)
