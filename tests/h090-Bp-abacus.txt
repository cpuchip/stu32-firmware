# worksheet-levels (a top level marked bare hidden, the mark set by clears and the fresh state and cleared by any op that makes the level). Same date, sources and grammar as h090.txt. Only the file for the option Michael picks is run.

V02Bp | abacus: a computed 0 in Y has a row and is made: drawn | STU 5 ENTER 5 - 7 | %LEVELS=YX %PAGEN=2
V03Bp | abacus: CLx: X is always drawn, the cleared levels above it are not | STU 5 ENTER 3 + CLX | %LEVELS=X %PAGEN=3 %PAGE-1=+|8.0000
V04Bp | abacus: a cleared Y under a full Z is drawn | STU 4 ENTER CLX ENTER 6 | %LEVELS=ZYX

V07Bp | abacus: the marks survive a save and load (T_BARE) | STU PRGM LBL:A 0 ENTER 3 RTN PRGM XEQ:A SAVE MEMCLR LOAD | %LEVELS=YX
V08Bp | abacus: a cleared stack saved and loaded stays cleared | STU 5 ENTER 3 + CLX SAVE MEMCLR LOAD | %LEVELS=X
